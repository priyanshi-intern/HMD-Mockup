Features

Safety that lives in the phone, not an app

Tap any feature to see how it works.

Starts with nothing

A phone with no social media, until you say otherwise

No browser. No social apps. Nothing, until you switch it on. Fusion X1 arrives as a blank slate, with the full Google Play Store sitting behind your approval. The controls live in the operating system itself, so there is nothing to uninstall and nothing to sidestep.

App management

Approve. Block. Hide. From your phone.

No two kids are ready for the same things at the same age. Add an app when you judge the time is right. Hide it again if it isn't working out. Loosen the reins as they earn it. It takes seconds, and it happens on your phone, not theirs.

Location safety

Know where they are, without asking

Set up to 10 safe zones: home, school, the pitch, a friend's house. Get a nudge when they arrive, and another when they leave. Location updates every 24 seconds, with history when you need to look back.

Calls & texts

A smaller circle, on purpose

Calls and messages go only to contacts you have approved, so you always know who they are talking to. Widen the circle as they grow. Hand over the keys entirely when you are ready.

Screen time

Set it once. Let the phone hold the line.

Manage screen time remotely and schedule School Mode so distractions switch off during lessons. You set the rules. The phone keeps them. Low battery alerts come to you, so you know before they go quiet.

Powered by Xplora

Made with people who have spent years keeping kids safe

Every control runs through the Xplora Guardian app on your own Android or iOS phone. Guardian costs £4.99 a month1. Without it, Fusion X1 is simply a very good Android phone, with no restrictions attached.

A phone they will actually want to carry

Smart Outfits clip on to change what the phone does: a gaming controller, a ring light for photos, or the Casual Outfit for the days it gets dropped. Behind it, a 108MP camera and a 50MP selfie camera.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Is the HMD Fusion X1 a kids' phone?

It is a full smartphone designed for growing independence. The safety controls let a parent or guardian shape how it is used, which makes it a strong first phone for a younger child, while the specifications and Smart Outfits keep it appealing to older teens who want more freedom.

How is this different from a parental control app?

A parental control app sits on top of the operating system and can usually be removed or worked around. On the HMD Fusion X1 the controls are part of the operating system itself. The phone also ships with no browser and no social media apps, so access is granted rather than taken away.

What can I control from my phone?

Which apps your child can install and see, who they can call and message, how much screen time they get, and when School Mode switches distractions off. You also get real-time location, safe zone alerts, location history and low battery notifications.

Do I need a subscription?

The parental controls require an active Xplora Guardian subscription, currently £4.99 per month. The app itself is free to download on Android and iOS, and you can purchase and manage the subscription inside it. A mobile subscription and SIM, nano-SIM or eSIM, are also required and are not supplied with the phone.

Does the phone work without the Xplora app?

Yes. Without the Xplora setup it works as a standard smartphone, with no restrictions and no subscription required.

Can the screen be repaired?

Yes. The HMD Fusion X1 uses a Gen2 repairable design, so the screen, back cover, battery and charging port are all built to be replaced. iFixit guides are available in select markets.

What is a Smart Outfit?

A clip-on accessory that changes what the phone does, not just how it looks. The range includes a gaming controller, a ring light for photos and a protective case.
