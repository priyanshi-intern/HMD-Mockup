Overview

The HMD Fusion X1, explained

This isn't a locked-down handset they'll outgrow in a year. It's a proper phone, with a 6.56in 90Hz screen, a 108MP camera, 128GB of storage and a 5000mAh battery that runs for up to 56 hours1. The difference is that it arrives with nothing on it, and you decide what goes on and when.

The controls sit deep in the system rather than in an app they can delete, so a browser and social media are blocked until you allow them. As they earn more trust, you hand over more: another app, a wider circle of contacts, a later bedtime.

Parental controls run on the Xplora Guardian Service, a paid monthly subscription3 you set up on your own phone once this one arrives. Without it, the Fusion X1 works as a standard HMD Fusion smartphone.

Who the HMD Fusion X1 is for

The HMD Fusion X1 is for the handover. It suits a first phone for kids at 10, 11 or 12, when they need to ring home from the bus but shouldn't have an open browser in their pocket, and it still suits a teenager two years later once most of the controls are off. Parents who want a kids phone without buying something childish will find a full Android smartphone wearing a blue case.

It's also for anyone who has tried a parental control app and watched it get uninstalled. These controls sit in the system. If you have been looking for a mobile phone for kids that you can actually manage, this is that kids phone.

It isn't for you if you want the controls free: they run on the Xplora Guardian Service, a paid monthly subscription3. It isn't for a child who only needs calls and texts either, since a feature phone does that for a fraction of the price.

FAQ

HMD Fusion X1 questions

Is the HMD Fusion X1 a kids phone?

Yes, and it's a full smartphone as well. HMD designed it for growing independence: a kids phone for a 10 or 11 year old getting their first one, with parental controls you relax as they get older, and a proper Android phone underneath for when they're a teenager.

What age is the HMD Fusion X1 for?

HMD doesn't set an age. In practice it suits the first-phone years from about 10 to 13, because you choose which apps appear and who they can call. For a younger child a basic feature phone is usually the easier starting point.

How much is the HMD Fusion X1 in Ireland?

The HMD Fusion X1 price is €249.99 SIM-free from the HMD shop, plus a monthly Xplora Guardian Service subscription if you want the parental controls.

Do I need a subscription for the parental controls?

Yes. The controls run on the Xplora Guardian Service, which you buy and manage in the Xplora app on your own phone after the Fusion X1 arrives. Without it the phone works as a standard HMD Fusion smartphone, with no restrictions.

Can my child get around the parental controls?

HMD builds the controls into the device software rather than an app, so they can't be uninstalled or switched off the way a third-party parental control app can. Access to a browser and social media is blocked by default until you allow it.

Can they use the internet and social media on it?

Only if you allow it. The phone has full Google Play access, but everything is blocked by default and each app has to be approved by you, including any browser or social app.

Can I see where my child is?

Yes. The Xplora Guardian app shows their live location, updating every 24 seconds, keeps a history with waypoints every five minutes, and lets you set up to 10 safe zones with alerts when they arrive or leave.

Is the HMD Fusion X1 SIM-free, and does it take an eSIM?

Yes, it's sold SIM-free confirm and takes either a nano SIM or an eSIM, so you can use any Irish network on 5G, 4G, 3G and 2G. A SIM and mobile plan are not included.

Does the HMD Fusion X1 come with a charger?

No. The box has a USB-C cable, a SIM pin and two Smart Outfits, but no wall plug. Any USB-C charger works, and the phone charges at up to 33W.

Can the screen be repaired?

Yes. HMD Gen2 repairability covers the display, back cover, battery and charging port, with repairs through GSMnet in select markets. HMD's self-repair page has the details.
