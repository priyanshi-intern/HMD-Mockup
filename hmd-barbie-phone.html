Overview

The HMD Barbie Phone, explained

No feeds, no apps begging for attention: just the people you want to talk to, and Malibu Snake for the bus home. It runs on 4G5 and talks for up to 9 hours1.

Outside, this Barbie flip phone is chic, sleek and pink, with a 1.77in display that doubles as a mirror. Inside, the 2.8in screen and Barbie-themed interface hide a few Easter eggs.

Then make it yours with the covers, stickers, gems and charms. It all arrives in a jewellery box: the rare phone that comes gift-wrapped.

Who the HMD Barbie Phone is for

It is for anyone who wants a basic phone with personality: Barbie fans, collectors, and anyone giving their smartphone a weekend off. If you want a pink flip phone that calls, texts and looks the part, this is it. At €59.99 it is a cheap flip phone that doesn't look it, and a simple phone that makes an easy gift.

Buying for a younger user? As a basic phone for kids or a dumb phone for kids, it covers calls and texts, with no social media and no web browser on its app list confirm. Check HMD's age guidance first confirm age: the box contains small parts. Teenagers get the same break from the feed, in a far better outfit than the average flip phone for kids.

It is not for you if you need WhatsApp, maps, a web browser or a proper camera. It runs S30+, not Android, and none of those apps is listed confirm.

FAQ

HMD Barbie Phone questions

What is the HMD Barbie Phone price in Ireland?

€59.99 at the HMD Shop, in Power Pink with 64 MB RAM and 128 MB storage. Delivery is free over €100, with 14-day returns confirm.

What is the HMD Barbie Phone?

It is the Barbie flip phone from HMD: a pink flip phone with 4G, made with Barbie. It is a basic phone for calls and texts, with a Barbie-themed interface, an outer display that doubles as a mirror, and covers, stickers and charms in the box. Some people search for it as the Nokia Barbie phone: it is made by HMD, the maker of Nokia phones.

Does the Barbie phone have WhatsApp?

No. It runs S30+, and WhatsApp is not among its apps confirm. The apps listed are Malibu Snake, Digital balance tips and Barbie meditation.

Does the HMD Barbie Phone support 5G?

No. It is a 4G flip phone, with 3G and 2G as well. For calls and texts, 4G is all it needs.

How long does the HMD Barbie Phone battery last?

Up to 9 hours of talk time on 4G, measured by HMD in a lab. The 1450 mAh battery is removable, so it can be swapped when it eventually wears out.

Does the HMD Barbie Phone come with a charger?

No. The box includes a USB-C cable but no wall charger, which HMD leaves out to reduce e-waste. Use a USB charger you already own.

Is the HMD Barbie Phone SIM free?

Yes, it is a SIM free flip phone at the HMD Shop confirm unlocked. It takes a nano SIM, so it also works as a pay as you go flip phone: just add a pay as you go SIM.

How much storage does the HMD Barbie Phone have?

128 MB of internal storage with 64 MB of RAM, and a microSD slot for cards up to 32 GB, sold separately. You need a card for the MP3 player.

Is the Barbie phone for kids?

Not specifically: HMD does not market it as a children's phone, so check HMD's age guidance first confirm. The box contains small parts. For a first smartphone with parental controls, look at the HMD Fusion X1.

Is the HMD Barbie Phone waterproof?

No water resistance rating is listed for it, so treat it as not waterproof and keep it away from the pool.
