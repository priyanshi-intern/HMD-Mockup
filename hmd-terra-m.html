Overview

The HMD Terra M, explained

Most phones don't last long on a building site. The Terra M is made for one. It survives accidental drops of up to 1.8m3, works from -25°C to 55°C4 and takes a high-pressure hose-down1. The 2.8in Gorilla Glass 3 touchscreen works with gloves and wet fingers, and there's a physical keypad for when touch won't do.

Press one key to talk to your team. Hold the other in an emergency. Dual nano-SIM plus eSIM puts two lines on one device, and the 2510mAh battery lasts up to 10 days on standby5. When it finally wears out, six screws on the back let you fit a new one.

It comes in Dark Grey, with 2GB RAM, 32GB of storage8 and a microSD slot for up to 1TB more. Fleets can enrol it in MDM and update it remotely with HMD FOTA, with five years of quarterly security updates7.

Who the HMD Terra M is for

The HMD Terra M is a rugged phone for people whose work is hard on phones: construction crews, security teams, first responders, healthcare staff, defence and critical infrastructure. It's a builders phone, a construction phone and a tradesman phone, and a tough mobile phone for anyone who needs to talk instantly and call for help fast. For IT teams, it's a rugged work phone that enrols in MDM and updates remotely through HMD FOTA7.

It isn't for you if you want a smartphone, 5G or a big screen for video. It's a rugged mobile phone first.

Push-to-Talk

Talk to the team. One press.

Press the programmable PTT key and the Terra M works like a walkie talkie phone, sending your voice to one person or a whole group through a Push-to-Talk app. The PTT app is pre-loaded, and managed services from Mobile Tornado, Eviden and Zello cover bigger teams. Some advanced PTT features need a paid subscription confirm price6. Need a shortcut instead? Program the key for that.

Emergency

Help is one key away.

The second programmable key is for emergencies. Switch on Emergency SOS, then hold the key for five seconds: the Terra M sounds a loud alarm, counts down and calls emergency services, and can text your location to chosen contacts. Or link it to your own safety app. An emergency button phone, ready when you are.

FAQ

HMD Terra M questions

How much is the HMD Terra M?

The HMD Terra M price is £189.99 at the HMD shop, with 2GB RAM, 32GB of storage and a Dark Grey finish. For fleet orders, contact HMD Secure.

Is the HMD Terra M waterproof?

Yes. The HMD Terra M is a waterproof phone rated IP68, so it survives up to 30 minutes at 1.5m deep, and IP69K, so it withstands high-pressure, high-temperature water jets. Dry it before charging, and don't charge it while the port is wet.

Is the HMD Terra M a smartphone?

No. The HMD Terra M is a rugged phone with a 2.8in touchscreen and a physical keypad, running Pulse OS. It has 4G, Wi-Fi, a web browser, maps and pre-loaded work apps, but it's built for calls, Push-to-Talk and tough conditions.

Does the HMD Terra M have Push-to-Talk?

Yes. It has a programmable PTT key and a pre-loaded Push-to-Talk app, so it works like a walkie talkie phone. Some advanced PTT features need a paid subscription confirm price, and managed PTT services are available from HMD partners.

What does the emergency key on the HMD Terra M do?

It calls for help. With Emergency SOS switched on confirm UK default, holding the key for five seconds sounds an alarm, starts a countdown and then calls emergency services. It can also send your location to chosen contacts, or you can link the key to a safety app.

How long does the HMD Terra M battery last?

Up to 10 days on standby, based on HMD's internal testing. The 2510mAh battery charges from empty to full in about three hours, and you can replace it yourself by removing six screws on the back.

Is the HMD Terra M a military grade phone?

Yes. It meets MIL-STD-810H, based on commissioned third-party testing, survives accidental drops of up to 1.8m and works from -25°C to 55°C. The screen is Corning Gorilla Glass 3.

Does the HMD Terra M have dual SIM?

Yes. This rugged phone takes two nano-SIMs and also supports eSIM, so you can keep a work line and a personal line on one device.

Does the HMD Terra M have 5G?

No. The HMD Terra M works on 4G, 3G and 2G, with VoLTE and Wi-Fi calling where your network supports them.

Does the HMD Terra M come with a charger?

No. The box includes a USB-C cable but no plug. A 5V 1A USB-C charger fills the battery in about three hours, or use the HMD Terra Charging Cradle, sold separately.
